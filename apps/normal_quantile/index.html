Find the quantiles of a normal distribution

For a normal distribution with mean μ and standard deviation σ, find the x values (quantiles) that have the given probabilities below them. For example, the 0.3 quantile is the value that has 30% of the distribution below it. Drag the slider or type the probabilities into the boxes.
